Privacy Policy

Effective 27 November 2026

Lyrpay is a multi-currency wallet run by Lyr Hub LLC, a New Hampshire limited liability company in Manchester, United States ("Lyrpay", "we", "us"). This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use lyrpay.com, the Lyrpay app and the Lyrpay Visa card, why we collect it, who we share it with and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address, password (stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider), mobile number and country.
  • Identity checks: your mobile number, which we confirm with a one-time code and a check of the kind of line it is.
  • Card application: if you apply for a Lyrpay Visa card, your legal first and last name, date of birth and billing address, and a record that you accepted the cardholder terms (including the date, IP address and browser).
  • Payout details: the bank, PayPal or other account details you give us to withdraw money.
  • Messages: what you write to our support team or to Lyrbot, our in-app assistant.

Information created when you use Lyrpay

  • Transactions: money you add, send, receive, convert or withdraw; gift cards; payments to Lyr apps; and Lyrpay card authorizations and purchases, including the merchant's name, category and location.
  • Device and usage: IP address, browser or app version, and the dates and times of sign-ins and requests, which our servers record in their logs.
  • Referrals and rewards: referral codes you used and the points and rewards you earned.

Information from others

  • Sign-in providers: if you sign in with Google or Facebook, they give us your name and email address.
  • Payment partners: confirmation of payments, payouts and card events from the partners listed in section 3.
  • Other Lyrpay users: your email or mobile number when someone sends you money.

We never see or store your full card number, expiry date or security code. On our pages these are shown and entered only in secure frames served by Stripe.

2. How we use it

  • To open and run your account, hold your balances and carry out the payments you ask for.
  • To issue and run your Lyrpay card, including approving each card payment against your balance.
  • To verify who you are, prevent fraud and keep accounts and money safe.
  • To meet our legal obligations, including anti-money-laundering, sanctions and tax laws, and to answer lawful requests from authorities.
  • To send receipts, security codes, tracking updates and important notices about your account. If you joined a waitlist, we email you when that product is ready.
  • To answer your questions and improve Lyrpay.

We do not sell your personal information, share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, or use it to build advertising profiles. Lyrpay has no third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

  • with a buyer or successor if our business is sold or reorganised, under this policy's protections.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. After you close it, we keep transaction and identity records for as long as financial laws require, usually at least five years, and then delete them. Server logs are kept for a shorter time.

5. Security

All traffic to Lyrpay is encrypted with HTTPS. Data is encrypted at rest on our hosting provider, and staff access is limited and logged. Card numbers stay with Stripe. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your password and devices safe and tell us at once if you suspect misuse.

6. Cookies and local storage

Lyrpay uses your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and to remember a referral code you arrived with. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Stripe sets its own cookies on its secure frames and checkout pages to prevent fraud.

7. Your choices and rights

You can view and update your account details in the app. You can also ask us to give you a copy of your information, to correct it, or to delete it (subject to the records we must keep by law). Depending on where you live, including California, the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, you may have further rights, such as to object to or restrict some uses of your data, to take your data elsewhere, or to complain to your local data protection authority. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

To make a request, email support@lyrpay.com from the address on your account. We may need to confirm your identity first, and we will reply within the time the law allows.

8. International transfers

Lyrpay is run from the United States, and our providers may process data in other countries. When we move personal information out of your country, we rely on safeguards the law recognises, such as standard contractual clauses.

9. Children

Lyrpay is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has given us their information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also tell you by email or in the app before it takes effect.
